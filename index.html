Clarity at every step

How BlockCert works.

No public registration. No uploaded certificate. Just transparent, on-chain checks.

01▤

An issuer prepares a credential

An authorized college admin selects a certificate document and enters its details.

02◇

A fingerprint is recorded

Your browser calculates the SHA-256 hash. The original file never leaves your device.

03▦

A shareable QR is ready

The student can share a QR code linked to that specific certificate’s public verification page.

04✓

Anyone checks the record

The app compares the uploaded certificate fingerprint with its on-chain record.

ⓘ

Demo, not production. BlockCert runs a real smart contract on a locally persisted blockchain. It listens only on your computer (127.0.0.1) and is not a public cloud deployment. Use sample or test certificate data only—never real students’ private documents or information.
